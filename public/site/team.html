Community Manager

Be the heart of the Jumpi community.

What you'll do

  • Run our Discord and community channels together with the team
  • Plan community events, contests and celebrations in the game
  • Lead the Guides and keep the community friendly and safe
  • Collect what players say and bring it to the developers

You're a great fit if

  • You've run or moderated an online community
  • You write clearly and calmly
  • You love games and care about kids being safe online

Who can apply: from age 16.

Apply as Community Manager

Guide

Guides are Jumpi's moderation team.

What you'll do

  • Keep an eye on the chat and keep it kind
  • Help players who are stuck, lost or upset
  • Welcome new players and answer questions
  • Tell the team about problems and bugs you notice

You're a great fit if

  • You're patient, friendly and stay calm
  • You know Jumpi, or love games like it
  • You can be online a few hours every week

Who can apply: from age 15.

Apply as a Guide

Beta Tester

Play new things before anyone else.

What you'll do

  • Try new updates, items and events early
  • Find bugs and tell us how to make them happen again
  • Share honest feedback
  • Test on your own phone or computer

You're a great fit if

  • You notice the little details
  • You can explain what happened step by step
  • You have an Android phone, an iPhone or a computer

Who can apply: from age 13.
