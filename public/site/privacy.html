    The short version (for players)

    • We only ask for a username, an email address, a password and your date of birth. We never ask for your real name, address, phone number, photos or where you are.
    • Other players see your username, your character and what you do in the game. They never see your email or password.
    • Only friends can send you private messages. Our filter checks messages to keep Jumpi friendly, and our team may read them if it's needed to keep players safe.
    • We don't show ads, we don't sell your information, and we don't use tracking cookies.
    • You or your parent can ask us at any time to see, fix or delete your information.

    This summary is here to help. The full policy below explains everything in detail.

    1. Who we are and what this policy covers

    This Privacy Policy explains how Jumpi Games, a sole proprietorship registered in Israel as an exempt dealer (Osek Patur) under number 328170832 ("Jumpi Games", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the Jumpi game, the Jumpi website and related services (the "Service"). It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

    For the purposes of the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 and its regulations, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), and other applicable data protection laws, Jumpi Games is the controller (owner of the database) responsible for your personal information. You can contact us, and our person responsible for privacy matters, at support@jumpigames.com.

    Because many of our players are children, this policy is written to be understood by parents and, as far as possible, by players themselves. Section 9 explains our approach to children's privacy in detail.

    2. Information we collect

    We follow the principle of data minimisation: we collect only what we need to run a safe, fun game. We collect the following categories of information.

    2.1 Information you give us when you sign up

    • Username, chosen by the player. It is public inside the game. Players are told not to use their real name.
    • Email address, used to identify the account, to contact the account holder or parent about the account, security and important changes, and to verify requests.
    • Password. We never store your password itself; we store only a one-way cryptographic hash of it (using the bcrypt algorithm), which cannot be turned back into the password.
    • Consent records: confirmation that the Terms and this policy were accepted, and confirmation regarding age or parental permission, together with the date and time of each confirmation.
    • Date of birth: asked when you sign up, to check your age (and whether a parent needs to agree) and to give you a little birthday present in the game. Other players never see it; on your birthday they only see a small cake next to your name.
    • Starting character choices, such as body colour, eye colour and first outfit.

    2.2 Information created while you play

    • Game profile and progress: your character's look, the Virtual Items you own, your coin balance, daily-gift streak, coins earned from mini-games each day, your home's layout and decorations, and account dates (such as when the account was created and last signed in).
    • Social connections: your friends list and pending friend requests.
    • Gameplay activity: your position and movement in game areas, emotes, game invitations, duel results and trades. Most of this is processed in real time and not stored after your session ends, except where needed for your account (for example, items received in a trade).
    • Public chat: messages you send in public chat are delivered in real time to nearby players. For safety and moderation, a copy (with the sender, the place and the time) is kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically. Messages blocked by our filter are kept the same way.
    • Trade and game history: a record of completed trades (who swapped which items) and one-on-one games (players, result and coins) is kept for one year, so that we can investigate problems, fraud or complaints.
    • Private messages (JumpiChat): messages between friends are stored in our database, together with the sender, recipient, time sent and whether they were read, so that players can see their conversation history.
    • Safety and moderation information: records of messages blocked by our filter, automatic or manual mutes, kicks, suspensions and bans, their duration and reason, and the moderator actions taken.
    • Preferences: language, sound and music volume, selected song, camera zoom and similar settings. Most of these are stored only on your own device (see Section 6).

    2.3 Information collected automatically

    • Technical and connection data: IP address, browser and device type, operating system, the pages and features requested, and the date and time of requests. We use IP addresses temporarily for security, for example to limit repeated sign-in attempts and to protect the Service from abuse.
    • Session cookie: a secure cookie that keeps you signed in (see Section 6).
    • Server logs: error and event logs (for example, that a moderator action took place, or that an account was automatically muted) used to operate, secure and fix the Service.

    2.4 Information from parents and support requests

    If you or a parent contact us, we collect the information included in the message (for example, name, email address and the details of the request) so that we can respond and keep a record of the request.

    2.5 What we do not collect

    We do not ask for or intentionally collect real names, home addresses, phone numbers, school names, photos, videos, voice recordings, precise geolocation or contact lists, and we do not receive or store card numbers. When a parent makes a purchase in the Jumpi Store, we keep a record of the order (which account, what was bought, the price, the date and the payment company's confirmation number); the card and billing details are entered on, and kept by, the payment company. We do not use advertising identifiers, third-party advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not build advertising profiles. Players are told not to share personal information, and our filter and moderators work to stop it; if we discover that such information was shared, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

    4. What other players can see

    • Everyone in the same game area can see your username, your character's look (including items you are wearing), your movements, emotes and public chat messages, and your status (for example, while you are typing or busy).
    • Players who view your player card can see your username and character, and can invite you to be friends, trade or play.
    • Your friends can see whether you are online and where you are in the game (for example, in the Plaza or at home), can visit your home, and can exchange private messages with you.
    • Players you trade or duel with can see the items you offer and the result of the game.
    • Other players can never see your email address, password, coin history or moderation records.

    5. Who we share information with

    We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only as follows:

    • Service providers who process information on our behalf and under our instructions, bound by contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations, including: our hosting provider, which runs our servers; and MongoDB, Inc. (MongoDB Atlas), which hosts our database.
    • Content delivery services: our pages load fonts from Google Fonts (Google LLC) and code libraries from jsDelivr. When your browser loads these files, those providers receive your IP address and basic browser information as part of the request. We do not send them any account information.
    • The payment company (currently HYP): when a parent buys something in the Jumpi Store, the payment is made on HYP's own secure page. We send HYP only the order number, the product and the price; HYP collects the card and billing details directly and handles them under its own privacy policy and the card industry's security rules.
    • Other players, to the extent described in Section 4.
    • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information if we believe in good faith that this is necessary to comply with law, regulation, legal process or an enforceable request from a competent authority; to protect the safety of any person, especially a child; to report suspected child exploitation or abuse to the relevant authorities or organisations; or to protect the rights, property and security of Jumpi Games, our players or the public.
    • Parents and guardians of a child's account, upon a verified request.
    • Business transfers: if Jumpi Games is involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganisation or sale of assets, information may be transferred to the successor, which will be bound by this policy or by protections at least as strong, and we will notify you of any change in who controls your information.

    7. How long we keep information

    We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law:

    • Account, game and social data: for as long as the account is active. If an account is not used for 24 months, we may close it and delete its data after trying to notify the account email address.
    • Private messages: for as long as both players' accounts exist, so that players can see their conversation history. When an account is deleted, the messages it sent and received are deleted.
    • Moderation and safety records: for as long as the account exists and for up to 12 months after a ban or the closing of the account, to prevent banned players from returning and to handle appeals, unless a longer period is required to protect a child or comply with law.
    • Public chat records: 30 days, then deleted automatically. Trade and game history: one year.
    • Server logs and IP addresses: for up to 30 days, unless needed longer to investigate a security incident or abuse.
    • Consent records and privacy requests: for as long as the account exists and for a reasonable period afterwards, as needed to show that we complied with the law.
    • Backups: deleted data may remain in secure backups for up to 90 days before it is overwritten.

    When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous so that it can no longer identify anyone.

    8. How we protect information

    We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration, including: hashing passwords with bcrypt; session cookies that page scripts cannot read; encryption of data in transit (HTTPS) on our live service; limiting how often sign-in and other actions can be attempted; validating all game actions on our servers; limiting access to personal information to authorised staff and moderators who need it for their role and who are bound by confidentiality; and keeping our systems up to date. We also apply the requirements of the Israeli Protection of Privacy (Data Security) Regulations, 5777-2017, to the extent applicable.

    No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will notify you, the relevant authorities, or both, as required by law.

    9. Children's privacy and parents' rights

    Jumpi is designed for children, and we take children's privacy especially seriously. We have designed the Service with privacy by default: we collect very little information, usernames must not reveal a real identity, private messages work only between friends who both agreed, chat is filtered, there is no advertising, and children's information is never sold.

    9.1 Parental consent

    A player under 18 may only use the Service with the permission of a parent or legal guardian. Where the law requires verifiable parental consent before we collect personal information from a child, including under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA") for children under 13, and under GDPR Article 8 and the UK GDPR for children under the age of digital consent in their country (between 13 and 16), we will obtain that consent before collecting more than the minimum information needed to request it.

    9.2 What we collect from children

    We collect from children only the information described in Section 2, and we do not require a child to disclose more information than is reasonably necessary to take part in an activity. We do not condition participation on providing more information than needed.

    9.3 Parents' rights

    A parent or legal guardian may at any time: review the personal information we hold about their child; ask us to correct or delete it; refuse to allow further collection or use of the child's information; and ask us to close the child's account. To make a request, email support@jumpigames.com from the email address linked to the account, or provide other information that allows us to reasonably verify that you are the child's parent or guardian. We will respond within the time required by law.

    9.4 If we learn we collected information without consent

    If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child without the consent required by law, we will delete it as soon as reasonably possible, and may close the account. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

    10. Your privacy rights

    Depending on where you live, you (or a parent or guardian acting for a child) have some or all of the following rights:

    • Access: to know whether we hold personal information about you and to receive a copy. Under section 13 of the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, you have the right to review information about you held in a database.
    • Correction: to ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete, unclear or out of date (section 14 of the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law).
    • Deletion: to ask us to delete your information and close your account.
    • Restriction and objection: to ask us to limit how we use your information, or to object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
    • Portability: to receive information you gave us in a structured, commonly used format.
    • Withdrawing consent: where we rely on consent, to withdraw it at any time, without affecting processing that took place before.
    • Human review: to ask for a person to review an automated moderation decision.
    • Complaint: to complain to a data protection authority. In Israel, this is the Privacy Protection Authority (Ministry of Justice). In the EU, it is the authority in your country; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

    To exercise any right, email support@jumpigames.com. We will need to verify your identity, and for a child's account that you are the child's parent or guardian, before acting on the request. We will respond within the period required by applicable law (generally within one month), free of charge, unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive. Residents of U.S. states with privacy laws may also have similar rights to know, delete and correct information; we will honour such requests as required by law. We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

    11. International transfers

    We are based in Israel. Our service providers may store or process information in other countries. The European Commission has recognised Israel as providing an adequate level of data protection. Where personal information of residents of the EU or UK is transferred to a country that has not received such recognition, we use appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. Transfers of information from Israel abroad are made in accordance with the Israeli Protection of Privacy (Transfer of Information to Databases Abroad) Regulations, 5761-2001.
