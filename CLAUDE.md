# Jumpi Games: notes for Claude

## Design rules (from Ilan)
- **No small "label" chips or kicker tags above or near headings.** No pills like "NEW", "Coming soon", "What's inside", "Weekly poll", "For parents", "Log out" sitting on top of a title. They look AI-made. Use the heading alone, with plain text under it if needed.
- Keep the look of the game: chunky rounded buttons with a darker bottom edge, Lilita One headings, Fredoka body text, bright game colours, and panels styled like the in-game windows.
- Prefer real 3D (the Jumpi character from `/blobby.glb`, three.js) or in-game art over generic emoji/icon illustrations.
- Avoid generic "AI" layouts (rows of identical cards with emoji icons). Design something that feels like part of the game.

## Practical
- Website lives in `public/site/` and is the home page (`/`, plus `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/contact`); the game is `public/index.html` at `/play`. Old `/site/...` links redirect.
- Hebrew is switched off in the game for now (`LOCKED` in `public/shared/i18n.js`); the default language is English.
- Contact: support@jumpigames.com · Business: Jumpi Games, exempt dealer (עוסק פטור) 328170832 · no address on the site.
- Plaza shops you can walk into (E at the door): furniture, clothes, dance club, restaurant; plus the Pet Center at the end of the Park. They are "places" built in `VENUE_BUILD` (index.html) and rooms `place:<id>` on the server.
- Needs (hunger/energy/stamina/fun) are counted on the server (`realtime/needs.js`); a full refill costs 25 coins. In the game the four gauges sit in a white bar tucked behind the minimap at the bottom right (`.needs`, follows the minimap with `--mm/--mr/--mb`; alone in the corner at home), the info popup opens above them.
- Pets: kinds and prices in `public/shared/pets.js` (shared by page and server); routes in `routes/pets.js`.
- Never start CSS class names or ids with "ad-", "ads", "banner", "sponsor" etc.: ad blockers hide those elements (this hid half of the pet adoption window).
- Jumpi Store (real money): products in `public/shared/store.js` (shared), page `/store` (`public/site/store.html`, `store-page.js`, `store3d.js`), server `routes/store.js` + `models/Order.js`. HYP card payments are NOT connected yet (`payments/hyp.js`, `ready()` returns false). To try purchases before that, set `STORE_TEST=1` in .env: admins get a "pretend to pay" button. Memberships never renew (User.memberUntil). Store-only auras: aura 8 (Sakura Breeze) and 9 (Crown of Stars), `exclusive` in catalog.js. **The store is closed for now** (`STORE_OPEN = false` in `public/shared/store.js`): `/store` shows the "under renovation" page (`public/site/store-soon.html/.css/.js`, two builder Jumpis in 3D), the game hides the offers button and checkout is refused. Set it to `true` to open the store again.
- Clothes (hair, shirts, pants, glasses, hats, necklaces) and their colours are listed once in `public/shared/outfits.js` (used by the game and by `catalog.js`). The place in the list is the item number, so only add new items at the end of a list.
- Any address that does not exist shows `public/site/404.html` ("Oops! Jumpi got lost", 3D lost Jumpi on a floating island, `404.css`/`404.js`); the catch-all is `notFound` at the end of server.js (files and scripts get a short "Not found" text instead).
- Saved accounts (up to 3 per device): the start screen shows the playing account in front and the others behind it, left and right, with their names; click one to swap (no password), "+" adds another (log in or new account). Server: httpOnly cookie `jumpi_accounts` + `/api/auth/accounts`, `/api/auth/switch` (routes/auth.js). Only accounts logged in with "Remember me" (or added with "+") are saved. There is no top "Log out" button any more: every name has an X. The X under the front character logs out (`/api/auth/logout`, the next saved one takes over); the X on one behind takes it off the device (`/api/auth/forget`). Characters behind never show their aura. Client: the `ACC` block in index.html (`accSync`, `accSwitch`, `accTick`).
- Invisible admins: admins come into the game invisible (`User.adminInvisible`, default true). Only other admins get their character/moves/chat/emotes/pet (shown half see-through); regular players get nothing and don't see them as online (`roomSend`/`sees` in realtime/plaza.js, `onlineWhere(id, seeHidden)`). In the game a purple bar at the top has "Show me" (socket `admin:invisible`); switch it on/off in the in-game admin panel (shield button, "INVISIBLE" section; `POST /api/admin/invisible` also exists for the website but has no button there). Client: `setGhost`, `setMyInvisible` in index.html.
- The game page blocks text selection (except inputs), the right-click menu and the F12 / Ctrl+Shift+I / Ctrl+U shortcuts. This only discourages; it can't truly stop someone opening the developer tools.
- Tags shop: Jumpi holds up a sign with the name and the tag being tried on (`nameSign` / `refreshMenuTag`).
- Mini-games open full screen (`mgOverlay`, `.mgx-*` in index.html): a start screen over the game playing itself (see-through), TUTORIAL (cards drawn with the game's own art) and PLAY, 3-2-1-GO, then a results card (stars from `MG_INFO[game].stars`, best score, coins). Four games: Fruit Catch, Shell Snap, Treasure Dig, Jumpi Flap (beach, Flappy-style: surfboards + golden shells). Server: routes/rewards.js (`/minigame/start|finish|dig|status`; scripted/validated scores, `User.gameBest`). `/play?mgtest` exposes `window.__openMg(id)` for testing.
- Houses: everyone starts with the plain room and buys upgrades with coins (Upgrade button in the home bar, owner only): Bigger Room 2,500, Garden 4,000, Swimming Pool 6,000 (needs the Garden), Second Floor 8,000. Prices and shapes are in `public/shared/houses.js` (used by the game and the server: `houseShape`, `spotProblem`). Saved in `User.house`, bought with `POST /api/home/upgrade`. The garden is in front of the room (furniture can go outside), the pool is swimmable (`homePool` in index.html), the second floor is a room of its own at x = 60 reached by the stairs in the back-left corner (`useStairs`). Plaza shops always use the plain room size (`setHouse(null)` in `enterPlace`). The server keeps per-home walking bounds (`homeBounds` in realtime/plaza.js).
- Time of day + weather: `public/shared/weather.js` works it all out from the clock (no server code): one game day = 24 real minutes, the weather (Sunny, Cloudy, Rain, Thunderstorm, Heat Wave) changes every 4 minutes with a 40-second blend, so every player sees the same. The game corrects its clock with the server's Date header (`wxSync`). Client: the `WX` block in index.html (`weatherTick` every frame: sky gradient, sun/moon position and shadows, stars, clouds, rain + splashes, lightning with screen flash, street lamps / shop windows / light pools at night, a soft light on the player in the dark, warm heat-wave and night tints). Sounds in the Sound module (`Sound.ambient`, `Sound.thunder`: rain, wind, thunder, crickets, birds, cicadas; muffled indoors). Clock pill at the top (`#wxClock`, click for the forecast card). `/play?wxtest` exposes `window.__wx(hour, kind)` to preview any time/weather.
- Jobs (phone → Jobs app): Waiter and Police Officer. Waiter (`public/shared/jobs.js`: 10 levels with titles, XP = orders served, pay per order 8 → 35 coins + a tip for fast service, shift 150 s, job pay capped at 800 coins a day, members 1,600). Server `routes/jobs.js`: `GET /api/jobs`, `POST /api/jobs/hire`, `/api/jobs/start` (one shift at a time, kept in memory), `/api/jobs/finish` (orders checked against the real time, at least 6 s per order). Saved in `User.jobs` (Mixed: {waiter:{hired,xp,served,shifts,earned}}), `User.jobsDay/jobsEarned`. Client: the `WORK` block in index.html: "GO TO WORK" takes you to a restaurant of your own (`venueWork`, socket room `work:<userId>`, bot customers): customers come in and wave (!), E = take order, the kitchen rings the bell and puts the plate on the counter with the table number, pick it up, bring it to that table. Patience ring on every bubble; out of patience = they leave and the order is cancelled. HUD with time / served / coins, start card and results card (level up). `/play?worktest` exposes `window.__work` for testing.
- Phone in hand is shared: opening/closing the phone emits socket `phone` (true/false); the server keeps `p.phone` (also sent in `join` and in `publicView`) and sends `player:phone` to the room, and other players see the same take-out / put-away animation with the phone in the hand (`remotePhone` in index.html).
- Police Officer job (kid-friendly: no weapons, nobody gets hurt): "START PATROL" takes off all your clothes and puts on the full police uniform (`buildUniform`/`setUniform`: shirt with badge, pockets and POLICE on the back, trousers, belt with buckle and radio, peaked cap with a gold star; kept out of the shop, `UNIFORM.parts` are only copied onto characters whose look has `uniform:"police"`). Other players see it: socket `uniform` → `player:uniform`, `p.uniform` in publicView/join, `uniformLook()` when building remote characters and you patrol the Plaza (real world, bots only on your screen). Cases (`POL` block in index.html, `policeTick` every frame): a pickpocket grabs a bag — chase him (he runs from you, stops now and then to catch his breath), tag him, he says sorry and hands it over, bring it back to the owner; a crying lost little Jumpi — find them, they follow you to their parent with the ? bubble. Arrows at the screen edge and dots on the minimap point to the cases. Pays 1.6x the waiter (`payMul`), at least 9 s per case on the server (`minSecs`), own titles Cadet → Legendary Chief. Going home / into a shop or "End patrol" ends it.
- Work uniforms are real clothes at the end of the lists in `public/shared/outfits.js` with `gift: "<job>"` (price 0): Police shirt / Police pants / Police cap (hat, hides the hair), Waiter vest & bow tie (shirt) / Waiter pants / Waiter apron (in Necklaces). They are never in the shop (`gift: true` in catalog.js, `/shop/buy` refuses, the client hides them) and can't be traded (realtime/trade.js). `JOB_LOOK` / `JOB_GIFTS` in public/shared/jobs.js: at work `setUniform(job)` wears those pieces instead of your clothes (other players see it: socket `uniform`, `uniformLook()`), and reaching the top level puts all of a job's pieces in the inventory (`/api/jobs/finish` → `gift`), each in its own category, to wear anywhere. 3D: `policeCap`, `policeBelt` (with the police pants), `waiterBow` (with the waiter shirt), `waiterApron`; textures `uniformShirtTex` etc.
- Season + Lucky Wheel (`public/shared/season.js`, server `routes/season.js`, client `SEA`/`WHL` blocks in index.html, star and wheel buttons on the left). Season: free 30-tier track, 100 XP per tier, XP from mini-games (+25), job tasks (+8 each), the daily gift (+40) and the wheel (+20), at most 320 XP a day (`addSeasonXp`, socket `season:xp` shows "+XP" and tier-ups). Each tier is claimed once (`POST /api/season/claim`): coins, and on tiers 5/10/15/20/25/30 season-only clothes (outfits.js `gift: "season:<id>"` + `tier`, never sold or traded). Seasons are listed in `SEASONS` (dates, colours, text); add the next one there and its 6 clothes at the end of outfits.js — after the last season ends a coins-only "Bonus Season" runs. Lucky Wheel: one free spin every 24 h from the last spin (`User.wheelAt`, `POST /api/wheel/spin`, the server picks the slice from `WHEEL` weights): coins, season XP, or rarely the Lucky Hat (`gift: "wheel"`; coins if already owned). Never for real money. User fields: `season` {id,xp,claimed}, `seasonDay`, `seasonDayXp`, `wheelAt`. The season window's header is a 3D banner (`CREW` block, `crewOpen`/`crewLoop`, canvas `#seaCrew`, own WebGLRenderer, only draws while the window is open): three Jumpis wearing this season's prize clothes, arms crossed (`crossArms`), jumping in and then breathing/swaying/nodding, on a twinkling star gradient with a shine sweep. The wheel has no card around it (`.whl-overlay` / `.whl-stage`, gold rim with chasing bulbs drawn in `drawWheel`). The wheel button (`#wheelBtn`, no `.btn` box) is a small golden wheel on the right under the settings button (lower for admins / when the offers button shows: `body.has-offers`); on hover it spins and the bulbs chase (`wbSpin`, `wbChase`), and it hides while the phone is open. The season star button is on the left. Red count badges on the side buttons sit half outside the button (`.btn .hb-badge`, `.btn .ph-badge`) so the icon keeps its size.
- Scrollbars: the website never shows any (`*{scrollbar-width:none}` at the end of site.css and legal.css). In the game the page has none, and scroll areas inside windows get a slim rounded blue thumb (global `::-webkit-scrollbar` rules near `.online` in index.html). The season prize track also scrolls left/right with the mouse wheel.
- Two accounts in two tabs of the same browser work: besides the login cookie, every game tab has its own token (`tabToken` in routes/auth.js, sent back by login / register / me / switch / logout; the game keeps it in sessionStorage `jumpiTab` via `TAB` and sends it as `x-jumpi-tab` in `api()` and as `auth.tab` on the socket). `currentUser` and the socket check it before the cookie. Safety net: the game also sends `x-jumpi-as: <its account id>`; `requireUser` (routes/shop.js) and the admin routes answer 409 `account-changed` if it does not match, and the window shows "ANOTHER ACCOUNT IS ON" (`#acctOv`) with a reload button. The website pages still use only the cookie.
- Pet things for the home (furniture 16-24 in catalog.js, `pet: true`, `PET_FURNITURE_FROM`): Pet Bed, Food Bowl, Water Bowl, Bouncy Ball, Chew Bone, Yarn Ball, Squeaky Duck, Cat Tree, Dog House. - Dance Club bar (`public/shared/bar.js` `BAR_MENU`, used by game + server): 8 drinks (juices, shakes, slushies) and 8 snacks (fries, edamame, chips, popcorn, nachos, nuggets, pretzels, fruit skewer), 8–20 coins, NO alcohol ever. The restaurant stays free. 3D models in `makeFood("club", i)`. Only add menu items at the end. `/play?bartest` exposes `window.__bar` {go, menu, song}.
- Club music: real audio tracks in `public/music/club/` (5 original dance tracks made for Jumpi by rendering them offline, so no copyright issues: Neon Bounce 126 bpm, Sunny Jump 108, Starlight Party 150, Disco Jumpi 118, Bubble Pop 124; ~2.5 MB mp3 each). The Sound module plays them shuffled in the club (`CLUB_TRACKS`, `clubPlay`, through `musicBus` so the music volume and on/off work), a toast says "Now playing: …" (`Sound.onClubSong`), and the dance floor lights follow the real beat (`Sound.clubBeat()`). The bar: order at the end of the counter ("ORDER AT THE BAR") or just sit on a stool ("SIT & ORDER" opens the menu; what you buy is served right there).
- Image studio: `/studio` (or `/play?studio`) opens a page of pictures made in code with the real 3D Jumpi (`STUDIO_SCENES` in index.html: each scene has `build()` returning {scene, camera, glow, vignette}; `studioRender(id, size)`). - Pizza Delivery job (`JOBS.delivery` in public/shared/jobs.js: 10 levels Rider titles, pay x2.6, at least 14 s per delivery, 240 s shift via `shift`/`shiftSecs`). "GO TO WORK" → `goDelivery()` takes you to Pizza Town: a whole town of your own (venue "delivery", `venueDelivery()`, room `work:<id>` with `work:"delivery"` → server `TOWN_BOUNDS` ±56). 5×5 road grid (`TOWN.R`), 56 numbered houses, a park, Jumpi Pizza (`buildPizzeria`, PICK UP window at `PIZZERIA.win`, scooter parking). Loop: E at the window = take a pizza (`dlvTake`, a bot customer waits in front of a random house, beam of light over it) → E at the scooter = ride (`dlvGetOn`; W/S gas/brake, A/D steer, or click a spot to drive there; camera swings behind) → E near the customer = deliver (`dlvDeliver`, pay + tip if faster than the par time) → back to the window. GPS on the right (`dlvGps`, heading-up map of the town, route along the roads with Dijkstra `townRoute`, turn instructions `gpsInstruction`) + a blue arrow line on the road (`dlvRibbon`). Uniform: Delivery shirt / shorts / cap (outfits.js `gift: "delivery"`, top-level prize). `/play?worktest` exposes `window.__dlv`.
