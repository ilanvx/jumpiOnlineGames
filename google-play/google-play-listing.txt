Welcome to JUMPI! Create your own little Jumpi and jump into a colourful 3D world full of things to do.

★ MAKE IT YOURS
Pick your colour and eyes, then dress up with hundreds of clothes: hairstyles, shirts, hats, glasses, necklaces and even legendary auras.

★ EXPLORE
Walk around the Plaza, splash in the Water Park and ride the giant slides, relax on the Beach, visit the Park, dance in the Dance Club and eat at the Restaurant.

★ PLAY & EARN COINS
Fruit Catch, Shell Snap, Treasure Dig and Jumpi Flap mini-games, a daily gift, a free Lucky Wheel spin every day and a Season Pass full of prizes.

Work as a Waiter, a Police Officer who finds lost kids and returns stolen bags, or a Pizza Delivery rider with your own scooter and GPS. Level up and win the full uniform!

★ YOUR OWN HOME
Decorate your house with furniture, add a garden, a swimming pool and a second floor, and adopt a pet that lives at home with you.

★ FRIENDS & SAFE CHAT
Make friends, trade items and challenge them to Tic-Tac-Toe and 4 in a Row. Private messages are for friends only, bad words and personal details (like phone numbers and addresses) are blocked automatically, and our team watches over the game.

No ads. Every update reaches you automatically.
